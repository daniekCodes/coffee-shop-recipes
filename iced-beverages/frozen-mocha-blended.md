# Frozen Chocolate Mocha

## Description

A cold blended coffee drink with espresso, chocolate, and milk.

## Ingredients

- 1 shot espresso
- 1 cup ice
- 3/4 cup milk
- 2 tbsp chocolate syrup
- Whipped cream
- Optional chocolate flakes

## How to Make Frozen Chocolate Mocha

### Step 1

Prepare one shot of espresso and allow it to cool for a moment.

### Step 2

Add the ice, milk, chocolate syrup, and espresso to a blender. Blend until smooth.

### Step 3

Pour the blended coffee into a large glass.

### Step 4

Step 4 is removed.
