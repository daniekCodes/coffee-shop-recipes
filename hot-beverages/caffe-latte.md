# Hazelnut Mocha

## Description: Chocolate and hazelnut coffee

## Ingredients

- 1 oz espresso roast
- 1 cup almond milk
- 2 tbsp chocolate syrup
- 1 tbsp hazelnut syrup
- Milk frother
- Optional whipped cream

## How to Make Hazelnut Mocha

### Step 1

Prepare the espresso and pour it into a large coffee mug.

### Step 2

Add the chocolate syrup and hazelnut syrup to the espresso and stir until combined.

### Step 3

Steam and froth the milk until there is a light layer of foam.

### Step 4

Slowly pour the steamed milk into the coffee while holding back the foam.

### Step 5

Add the remaining milk foam on top.

### Step 6

Optionally top with whipped cream and a small amount of chocolate syrup.
