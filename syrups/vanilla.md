# Simple Syrup

## Description: A sweet vanilla syrup for adding flavor to coffee drinks

## Ingredients

- 8 oz Cold Brew
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1 vanilla bean (or 1 1/2 Tbsp vanilla extract)
- A pinch of salt

## How to Make

### Step 1

- Cut the vanilla bean in half lengthwise. If using vanilla extract, save it until the end.

### Step 2

- Add sugar, water, and vanilla bean to a small saucepan. Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat.

### Step 3

- Allow the syrup to cool. Remove the vanilla bean and stir in vanilla extract if using.

### Step 4

- Store the syrup in an airtight container for up to two weeks.
