# Caramel Syrup Recipe

## Description

A simple homemade caramel syrup for adding a sweet caramel flavor to coffee, iced drinks, and other beverages.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## How to Make

### Step 1

Add the sugar and half a cup of water to a small saucepan. Heat over medium heat and stir gently until the sugar has dissolved.

### Step 2

Continue heating until the mixture develops a golden caramel color.

### Step 3

Carefully add the hot water and stir until the caramel has completely dissolved.

### Step 4

Remove from the heat and add the vanilla extract and a small pinch of salt.

### Step 5

Allow the syrup to cool before transferring it to a clean bottle or container.

## Serving Suggestion

Add 1-2 tablespoons to a latte, iced coffee, or cold brew.
